# Rendered inspection

Inspect the realized surface in its actual medium, not only its source or a screenshot captured at one convenient size.

1. Identify the source revision, implementation revision, medium, dimensions, device/input modes, and inspection date.
2. Check the intended journey, hierarchy, typography, layout, color, imagery, diagrams, content visibility, and text fallbacks against the source meaning.
3. Exercise relevant interaction, loading, empty, error, focus, hover, pressed, disabled, and reduced-motion states. Omit states that are not part of the surface and record that boundary.
4. Check reflow or adaptation at ordinary and constrained sizes, readable labels, focus visibility, contrast, target size, keyboard or equivalent input, and other presentational accessibility obligations material to the medium.
5. Record visual or interaction findings separately from meaning-changing findings. Return the latter to the owning authority before claiming render readiness.

The result is evidence for this realization only. It does not authorize publication, release, or a semantic edit.
