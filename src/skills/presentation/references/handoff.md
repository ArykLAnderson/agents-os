# Presentation handoff

Use this compact boundary when Presentation receives a governed source or brief.

## Entry routing

Choose the route before extracting requirements. Do not invent a revision where the source has none.

| Entry path | Meaning authority | Revision or provenance | Required handoff fields | Ambiguity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Document handoff | The named Document | Exact Document identity and semantic revision | Document handoff; audience/task; must-preserve claims; semantic hierarchy; meaning-bearing visual specifications; text alternatives; constraints | Return the bounded question to the Document. |
| Cases or Frames | The named current Case or Frame | Exact Case/Frame identities and revisions, plus source locators where material | Audience/task; accepted or provisional status; must-preserve requirements; semantic hierarchy; visual specifications; text alternatives; constraints | Return to the owning Case or Frame; preserve uncertainty until it is resolved there. |
| Existing product surface | The named product authority; otherwise the declared maintenance scope limits meaning | Surface locator and observed implementation revision or environment/date | Requested change or check; observed behavior; implementation boundary; applicable product requirements; states; constraints; inspection boundary | Return to the product authority. A check or fidelity refinement does not infer unstated product meaning. |
| Supplied brief | The brief's named author or requester for its declared outcome | Exact supplied text with any provided version/date; otherwise record its receipt as provenance rather than fabricating a revision | Brief; audience/task; requested outcome; known constraints; required states; inspection boundary; explicit unknowns | Return to the brief owner or preserve the ambiguity. |

## Source meaning

Record the route authority and source identity, revision, or provenance, audience and task, intended journey, semantic hierarchy, claims or requirements that must remain unchanged, meaning-bearing visual specifications, text alternatives, content-accessibility requirements, and known omissions or uncertainty.

## Realization brief

Record the medium and target surfaces, implementation constraints, interaction states, responsive or adaptive expectations, presentational accessibility expectations, imagery and asset limits, motion preferences, and inspection boundary. These are realization inputs, not new semantic claims.

## Return path

- Purely visual or interactive issue: refine the Presentation realization.
- Meaning-changing pressure: return a bounded question and evidence to the Document, Case, Frame, product authority, or supplied brief that owns the meaning.
- Source meaning changed: obtain a new source revision, invalidate affected realization and inspection evidence, and re-run the relevant checks.

Do not use a visual treatment, implementation convenience, or publication destination to settle a semantic disagreement.
