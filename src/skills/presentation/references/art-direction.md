# Art Direction lens

Use this optional Presentation lens when a deliberate visual point of view would improve the surface. It sharpens visual intent and useful variation without becoming a semantic or implementation owner.

## Shape the lens

Start from the Presentation task, audience, medium, and constraints. Infer a compact brief or accept one supplied by the user. Set explicit dials when they help comparison:

- **Variance:** familiar and restrained ↔ distinctive and surprising;
- **Motion:** still and quiet ↔ expressive and transitional;
- **Density:** spacious and sparse ↔ layered and information-rich.

The dials describe a range, not a scorecard or fixed aesthetic ban. Keep the chosen point of view legible in composition, typography, imagery, motion, and rhythm, while preserving the user's task and the medium's limits.

## Use references

Use a small set of references and anti-references when they clarify intent. For each, name what to borrow or avoid—such as pacing, contrast, framing, material feeling, or information density—not a framework, library, brand, or copied surface. An anti-reference is a warning about a failure mode, not a universal prohibition.

## Return the lens

Record one concise lens:

- **Surface and source:** medium, audience, task, source revision or provenance, and constraints.
- **Intent:** the visual point of view and the user outcome it serves.
- **Dials:** selected variance, motion, and density ranges, with reasons.
- **References:** useful references and anti-references, each with the trait to borrow or avoid.
- **Exclusions:** treatments rejected because they obscure meaning, harm access, or do not serve the medium.
- **Inspection questions:** what the rendered review must reveal about hierarchy, rhythm, motion, density, and distinction.

Presentation decides how to realize the lens and whether it serves the surface. Motion yields to reduced-motion needs; density yields to comprehension and accessibility; visual distinctiveness does not obscure source meaning. Meaning-changing pressure returns through Presentation to the relevant source authority.
