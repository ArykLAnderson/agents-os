# Review

Select only lenses justified by current risk and representation:

- **Case fidelity:** claims, scope, authority, inference, disagreement, and support remain faithful to Cases.
- **Fresh-reader comprehension:** grounding, order, terminology, assumptions, and reader action work without hidden context.
- **Genre:** the selected genre's semantic obligations are fulfilled.
- **Editorial quality:** specificity, economy, coherence, voice, and claim strength fit the audience.
- **Content accessibility and structure:** heading order, labels, text equivalents, semantic order, and other meaning-bearing access requirements are clear and support the intended reader action.

Use fresh-context reviewers when available. Findings identify severity, exact location, consequence, evidence, and correction. Consolidate duplicates without averaging away disagreement. Presentation owns rendered hierarchy, responsive behavior, typography, visuals, links, attachments, target behavior, and presentational accessibility; its findings remain separate unless they reveal meaning-changing pressure.

Semantic findings return to compose, shape, trace, or Frame. Handoff or meaning-changing findings return to the relevant source authority. Representation-only editorial findings return to format. Re-run only affected lenses after material revision.

Review completes when material findings are resolved, accepted as limitations, or clearly require human judgment.
