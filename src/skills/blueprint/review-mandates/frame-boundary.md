# Frame boundary review mandate

**Use:** a coherent external boundary with material ambiguity or affected parties; skip for a plainly direct, low-consequence boundary.

Challenge only: recognizable outcome/consumer; external guarantee vs internal-mechanism separation; scope/exclusions; authority-dependent questions; terrain evidence/limitations; and whether uncertainty could change the boundary. Do not propose module structures, route plans, new gate matrices, or generic best practices as requirements.

Output advisory findings with severity, evidence, confidence, affected boundary claim, proposed classification, and suggested correction. Do not accept or reject the Frame. Design/Frame reconciliation evaluates findings against current goals and phase; only Frame or human authority changes the boundary.
