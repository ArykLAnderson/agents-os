# Materialized Production Design RFC review mandate

**Use:** after a routeable Design/RFC Case is materialized and before accepting its direct/focused/full RFC projection. Do not use prose review as the working design loop.

Challenge both semantic fidelity and explanatory cohesion. A cold reader must be able to retell one causal spine: situation and tension → decisive architectural move → consequent owners/boundaries → one complete instance story → one revealing stress/recovery story → realization route → exact acceptance decision. Every primary section must answer a question created by the previous one; coverage categories, trace machinery, and visual metadata must not displace that story. Every retained visual specification must answer a distinct reader question and must not duplicate prose, another visual, or imply false topology. Use [Document](../../document/SKILL.md) for semantic and content-accessibility fidelity. When a rendered surface is in scope, use [Presentation](../../presentation/SKILL.md) for render-readiness checks at the requested medium and boundary. Destination and publication checks remain in Document's Publish path. Rendered findings do not become semantic requirements unless reconciled by the owning authority. Then challenge: one recognizable outcome; faithful external boundary; one selected internal design; canonical state/mutation/contract authority; immediate-consumer seam walked through analytically and exercised at the real consumer only when decision-changing evidence requires it; material alternatives and limitations; a current independently reconciled [Requirement-Killer Pass](../references/requirement-killer.md) whose design and route applications removed or deferred unsupported machinery and route burden, justified every retention, and reran consumer, failure, and fresh-worker walkthroughs; a route with no architecture invention; and proportional rigor. Correctness review must not impersonate or waive the opposing simplification mandate. Report advisory findings with evidence, confidence, affected claim, and proposed classification; do not accept, reject, or set readiness. Do not turn a preferred pattern, checklist, reviewer consensus, or final-product posture into acceptance criteria.

Design must reconcile all findings against the accepted Frame, current goals, phase, threat model, evidence, and selected architecture by updating affected atomic Design/RFC Case claims first. Allowed dispositions are immediate boundary violation/conformance repair; seam required now; evidence required now; deferred enforcement/hardening; design or behavior proposal; and irrelevant/invented requirement. Rematerialize the RFC after semantic changes. Never patch prose as an independent authority; only reconciled Case meaning may change the projection or readiness.
