# Materialized Production Design RFC review mandate

**Use:** after a routeable Design/RFC Case is materialized and before accepting its direct/focused/full RFC projection. Do not use prose review as the working design loop.

Challenge: one recognizable outcome; faithful external boundary; one selected internal design; canonical state/mutation/contract authority; immediate-consumer seam exercised; material alternatives and limitations; a current independently reconciled [Requirement-Killer Pass](../references/requirement-killer.md) whose design and route applications removed or deferred unsupported machinery and route burden, justified every retention, and reran consumer, failure, and fresh-worker walkthroughs; a route with no architecture invention; and proportional rigor. Correctness review must not impersonate or waive the opposing simplification mandate. Report advisory findings with evidence, confidence, affected claim, and proposed classification; do not accept, reject, or set readiness. Do not turn a preferred pattern, checklist, reviewer consensus, or final-product posture into acceptance criteria.

Design must reconcile all findings against the accepted Frame, current goals, phase, threat model, evidence, and selected architecture by updating affected atomic Design/RFC Case claims first. Allowed dispositions are immediate boundary violation/conformance repair; seam required now; evidence required now; deferred enforcement/hardening; design or behavior proposal; and irrelevant/invented requirement. Rematerialize the RFC after semantic changes. Never patch prose as an independent authority; only reconciled Case meaning may change the projection or readiness.
