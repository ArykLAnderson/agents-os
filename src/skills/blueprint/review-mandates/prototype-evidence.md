# Prototype evidence review mandate

**Use:** assembly/end-to-end/boundary-envelope prototypes, or a result that selects state/authority/external-mutation/restart/security design.

Challenge: is the question singular and decision-changing; is the discriminator observable; did the environment actually represent the claimed seam; does the observation support only the stated verdict; are limits/fakes/cleanup clear; and is code kept out of promotion pressure? Do not demand production polish, broad tests, or retention unless they are the experiment's proposition.

Output advisory evidence-quality findings and a recommended proposition classification of `supported`, `refuted`, or `inconclusive` within stated limits. This classifies evidence only; the reviewer does not reject the prototype direction, RFC, architecture, or route.

Do not treat absence of a production-ready seam, public endpoint, isolation mechanism, or hardening control as prototype failure when creating or testing that candidate seam is the proposition. Distinguish: evidence that refutes feasibility; a seam the prototype must expose; deferred production enforcement; and an invented requirement outside the evaluator. Design reconciliation owns the final disposition against current goals and phase.
