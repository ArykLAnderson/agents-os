# Validation

Validate structural and traceability integrity, not objective truth.

Check every Case for:

- required canonical aggregate fields and content families from the Case contract, independent of whether transport was JSON, draft, or direct flags;
- unique and stable Case, entry, and source IDs;
- entry purpose, classification, substantive content, and support;
- pinpoint support where the source is not short and uniformly supportive;
- authority for accepted authority-dependent meaning;
- visibly disclosed inference and quotations;
- at least two separately supported contested positions;
- successor and basis for superseded entries;
- resolvable Case, source, entry, artifact, and relationship targets;
- content that still fits the title, summary, and scope;
- cohesion across subject, authority, lifecycle, retrieval, and distribution boundaries.

The ordinary public CLI has no mechanical-repair command. Report a mechanical defect, or reconcile the complete Case only when the needed change is semantically justified. Never edit storage files as a repair shortcut, select another store, or silently change semantic meaning, classification, provenance, or human authority.
