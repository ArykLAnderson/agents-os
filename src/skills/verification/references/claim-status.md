# Evidence support

These labels describe evidence support. They do not change decision, planning, or document lifecycle state.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `observed` | Directly inspected source, output, provider state, or rendered behavior. |
| `inferred` | A conclusion reasoned from observations but not directly exercised. |
| `implemented` | The identified candidate contains the change. |
| `verified` | The declared behavior was exercised against the identified candidate through a relevant interface. |

Use a label only when support could otherwise be mistaken. Cite authority by owner and revision instead of treating acceptance as an evidence label. Report blockage as a result condition. When a material change or contradiction invalidates earlier evidence, retain its locator, name the invalidator, and reassess the claim.
